Functions are deployed with region `us-central1` and custom resource limits (`timeoutSeconds: 60–120`, `memory: 256MB`). The church list cache relies on a Firestore lock document `_master_churches_list_lock` and a stale threshold of 10 minutes (`STALE_MS`) plus a minimum 45-second recomputation cooldown (`RECOMPUTE_MIN_MS`). A cron-style scheduled function runs every 60 minutes to recompute the index.
