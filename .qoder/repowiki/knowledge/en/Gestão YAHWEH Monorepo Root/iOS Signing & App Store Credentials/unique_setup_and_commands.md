Secrets are prepared locally on Windows with `scripts\encode_ios_codemagic_secrets.ps1` (Base64-encodes P12/profile for Codemagic paste) and `prepare_codemagic_paste_from_bootstrap.ps1` (converts bootstrap output into Codemagic-ready text files). PEM mismatch recovery uses `scripts\gen_ios_distribution_csr_private_key_pem.ps1`. Profile/P12 consistency can be verified locally via `scripts\ios_verificar_perfil_com_p12.ps1` or `scripts/ios_verify_profile_p12.py`.
