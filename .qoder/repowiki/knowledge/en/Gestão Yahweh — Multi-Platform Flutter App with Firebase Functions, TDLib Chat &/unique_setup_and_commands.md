Deployment is orchestrated through `scripts/deploy_completo.ps1` and related helpers (`build_android_aab.ps1`, `deploy_web_hosting.ps1`, `regras_gcp_automatico_forcado.ps1`). iOS builds require Apple signing certificates in `IOS/` and CodeMagic configuration in `codemagic.yaml`. TDLib requires `TELEGRAM_API_ID` and `TELEGRAM_API_HASH` in `.env` (see `flutter_dotenv` loading in `tdlib_credentials.dart`). Firebase rules/functions are deployed via `firebase.json` with hosting public at `flutter_app/build/web`.
